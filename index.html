Ask Glow a question →
What types of treatments does Glow offer?

Glow lists dermatology, laser hair removal, skin rejuvenation, aesthetic treatments, hair restoration, body contouring and wellness services. Visit the services page for the current treatment range.

Where are Glow's clinics located?

Glow lists locations in Al Barsha 1, BurJuman and Deira City Centre in Dubai, as well as Sahara Centre in Sharjah. Branch contact details are provided above.

How do I arrange a consultation?

You can contact Glow through its contact page or call the published clinic number. Ask the team about the service you are interested in and the branch you would prefer.

How do I know which treatment is suitable for me?

Treatment suitability depends on individual circumstances. Discuss your concerns, medical history, possible risks and expected outcomes with an appropriately qualified medical professional before proceeding.

Are opening hours the same at every location?

The published hours vary by branch. Contact your chosen location before travelling to confirm its current opening hours and appointment availability.
