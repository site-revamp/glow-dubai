# Glow Dubai – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Glow Dubai, operated by Glow Aesthetics Dermatology Clinic, is a multi-location medical and aesthetic clinic serving Dubai and Sharjah. Its services include dermatology, laser hair removal, skin rejuvenation, cosmetic treatments, hair restoration, body contouring and wellness therapies.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating Glow's medical and aesthetic expertise, simplifying treatment discovery and making it easier for visitors to explore services and connect with their preferred clinic branch. The design direction prioritises a professional healthcare identity, clear information architecture and consultation-focused navigation.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three key issues identified on the original website:

### 1. Unfocused First-Impression Messaging
- Replaced the generic opening message, "How Can I Help You?", with more purposeful messaging that introduces Glow's dermatology, aesthetic and skin-care services.
- Strengthened the homepage introduction to communicate the clinic's core offerings and positioning.
- Made the primary messaging more relevant to visitors seeking professional skin and aesthetic treatments.

### 2. Fragmented Treatment Discovery
- Reorganised the treatment portfolio into clearer categories covering dermatology, laser, aesthetics, hair and wellness.
- Improved the presentation of services to help visitors explore treatments according to their individual concerns.
- Established a more intuitive content hierarchy to reduce the effort required to find relevant treatment information.

### 3. Insufficiently Consolidated Credibility and Branch-Specific Conversion Information
- Improved the organisation of medical-team information, clinic locations and contact details.
- Presented branch information more cohesively to help visitors identify their preferred clinic.
- Strengthened the connection between professional credibility, branch selection and consultation or appointment pathways.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more polished and professional healthcare aesthetic appropriate for a dermatology and medical aesthetics clinic.
- **Layout and Structure:** Improved section organisation and content hierarchy to accommodate Glow's broad treatment portfolio.
- **User Experience:** Created a clearer journey from the homepage introduction through treatment discovery, medical-team information and branch-specific contact options.
- **Content Consistency:** Improved the organisation and presentation of treatment categories, professional information and clinic details.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a more cohesive homepage structure that better communicates Glow's medical services and multi-location presence.

## Project Summary

The redesigned homepage addresses three key challenges: unfocused introductory messaging, fragmented treatment discovery and insufficiently consolidated credibility and branch information. The result is a more structured and informative digital experience that communicates Glow's healthcare offering more clearly while supporting treatment exploration and consultation enquiries.
